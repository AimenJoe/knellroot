## 공통
```
Attached: front and back renders of OUR PLAYER CHARACTER (a small raccoon cub wanderer in a purple hood and short ragged purple cape,
leather straps, short legs, striped tail). KEEP THIS CHARACTER EXACTLY AS IT IS — same head, hood, face, body, proportions, colors and tail.
Only ADD the one garment described below, worn OVER the existing outfit. Do not redesign the character, do not add weapons or held props.

[PURPOSE] A concept sheet for a 3D game garment that will be modeled and attached to this character. Show the SAME character twice
side by side: LEFT = straight front view, RIGHT = straight back view. A-pose-ish relaxed stance, arms slightly away from the body.
Style: stylized low-poly 3D game model with a hand-painted texture, matching the attached renders. Few big simple forms, chunky,
a strong clear silhouette that still reads when the character is only 40 pixels tall. Details are PAINTED as large flat color areas.
Each material uses only 2 or 3 flat tones. Flat, even, shadowless lighting. No outlines, no text, no logo, plain white background.

[WORLD RULES] "Color is life": living, worn, hand-made things are warm and soft; only the corrupting purple roots are sharp and angular
(so the garment must have ROUNDED, soft forms — no spikes, no armor plates with sharp edges). Never use red.
The garment was LEFT BEHIND by people who abandoned an underground village — it should feel old, handmade, lived-in, quietly heroic.
Make it look COOL and memorable, not like generic RPG armor.
```

## cloak
```
[GARMENT] "Worn Pilgrim's Mantle" — LIGHT. A long travelling mantle in pale dusty oat / sand color draped over the shoulders on top of
the purple hood-cape, falling almost to the ankles, its hem frayed into a few big soft tatters that sway when running. Closed at the
chest with a small dull-bronze clasp shaped like a little bell (a bell that does not ring). On the back: a soft rolled blanket strapped
across the shoulders and a few faded travel patches. The silhouette should feel like a lone pilgrim who walked very far.
```

## bark
```
[GARMENT] "Root-bark Mantle" — HEAVY. A short, thick shoulder mantle made of overlapping ROUNDED plates of dry tree bark, like big soft
pine-cone scales, in warm chestnut and honey-brown (living wood color, NOT purple), layered over both shoulders and down the upper back,
bound together with pale twine knots. A few small tufts of soft green moss grow between the plates. It makes the small character look
sturdy and rooted, like a little tree stump guardian. Rounded edges only.
```

## apron
```
[GARMENT] "Scorched Leather Apron" — MEDIUM. A heavy blacksmith/hearth-keeper's leather apron in warm dark brown, covering the front of
the body from chest to knees, its lower hem darkened with soot and a few small amber-orange ember scorch marks. Thick leather straps go
over the shoulders and CROSS in an X on the back, held by a round brass ring. One rounded leather pad on the right shoulder. It should feel
like the clothes of someone who tended a fire for a long time — warm, practical, a little brave.
```
