Knellroot /ˈnɛl.ruːt/

Beneath the earth a bell is ringing. No one rings it.
Wherever the roots reach, the dead rise.

A small hooded raccoon, a wanderer, follows the sound down.

Two views, one fight

Sweep through hordes in a quarter view. Lock on to the guardian blocking the way and the camera drops over your shoulder. Read the tells, dodge, parry, punish the opening.

Who makes it

Made by one person at Beardworks. The source code is public on GitHub. It is set in the same world as our first game, Naïl: Under the Roots, long, long after.

The 3D models of the wanderer, husks, thorn beast and guardian were made with Meshy (the husks, thorn beast and guardian from pictures drawn with a Google Gemini image model), then reduced and touched up (Meshy free-plan output, CC BY 4.0: “Hooded Raccoon Rogue”, “Crystal Gloom Bear”, “Crystalback Piglet”, “Sleepy Stone Guardian”). Some sound effects are CC0 sounds from Kenney and others; the font is Gowun Batang (SIL OFL 1.1). Full credits in LICENSES.
